Each new family has three named forms, a special, a six-node awakening tree, an art identity, and support for normal progression, equipment and battles. Existing characters receive more distinctive headgear, facial landmarks and silhouettes. City structures have redrawn pixel architecture while retaining their map positions.

## Currency and odds

- One Golden Orb costs 60 gems; ten cost 600 gems. Buying is a separate explicit action.
- A Golden summon consumes one Golden Orb; ×10 consumes ten. It never spends normal Spin Orbs or extra gems.
- Each Golden family has a 10% chance, independent of focus and daily luck.
- Stars follow the existing one-tier-above-owned cap and star weighting. A never-owned Golden family starts at 1★. Higher stars become available after ownership.
- Golden ×10 has no bonus guarantee or legendary-piece drop. The UI states this. Existing normal summon guarantees and piece drops remain in place.
- Older saves default to zero Golden Orbs. Export/import retains this currency and the new fighters.
- The historical 18-normal-fighter collection achievement remains earnable at 18; previously claimed rewards are preserved.

Collection, normal summon focus, and Hall collection pages initially display 10 entries, with Show all / Show fewer controls. The Golden pool contains exactly 10 entries.
